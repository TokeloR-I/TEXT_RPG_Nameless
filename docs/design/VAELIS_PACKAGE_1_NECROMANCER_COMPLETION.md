# Vaelis — Package 1: Necromancer Completion Contracts
**Authority:** Creative Director delegated gameplay numbers to Vaelis (2026-10-23). **State:** Approved design, implementation in separate developer PRs. Preserve existing Soulbinder N-3/N-4 contract and QA-8A one-resource rule. Source reference: `src/data.js` and `docs/design/NECROMANCER_DECISION_REGISTER.md`.

## N-5 Possession — `control_enemy`
- Existing **3 Soul Energy**, one living enemy, **one enemy normal turn opportunity**. One possessed target per caster; recasting replaces previous control. Cannot target bosses, explicitly control-immune enemies, or enemies at **75+ CR** (Package 2); elite non-bosses are eligible below that threshold.
- On cast, consume caster action and resource only if target is legal. Record original faction, initiative slot, source caster and one-turn expiry. Target retains its normal initiative position, HP, stats and resource; no duplicate player or enemy turn.
- On the controlled enemy's next normal turn, the **player chooses one legal action and a legal target** from that actor's existing action list, subject to the actor's own costs, cooldowns, target rules and immunities. During this action only, the controlled enemy may target its former allies as opponents and support the player's team; never self-target with a hostile action. If no usable action exists, it takes a no-op and the control expires. The caster cannot use boss-exclusive moves or invent abilities.
- If target is stunned/skipped, its opportunity is consumed and Possession ends without an extra action. Death, cleanse and combat end end control immediately. On expiry restore original faction and AI, not lost HP/resource. A possession-generated kill counts as the **controlled actor's** kill, not as the Necromancer's own direct kill or Soul Energy source. No possession recursion.
- **Engine handoff to Keel:** must expose pending controlled action via engine-owned `getAvailableActions()/act()` equivalent; UI never runs combat rules. Until player choice can be represented in both CLI and GUI, do not ship an invisible automatic enemy action as a substitute.
- Tests: normal/elite/boss; 74 vs 75 CR; dead/cleanse; skipped turn; zero legal actions; legal target inversion; original initiative preserved; player choice from both interfaces; snapshot/restore; refusal does not spend action/energy.

## N-1 March of Endless Bones — `resurrect_all_minions`
- Preserve existing **once/fight, cost 'all' Soul Energy**, requiring at least 1 energy. Records are **friendly summons that died in this fight only**; no enemy corpses, no previous encounters. Ledger tracks original summon type, owner, max HP, death order and unique ID. An already resurrected entry cannot be resurrected twice from one death event.
- On cast: reserve capacity under the **current living minion cap** (base 3, Black Pharaoh +3); restore fallen summons **oldest death first**, each at **ceil(50% max HP)**, without prior buffs/debuffs, as a new living instance linked to original owner; they first act **next normal round**. After resurrecting, add **one free Ghoul** if a slot remains, using its existing template/damage override, also joining next round. No cap overflow and no extra action. If capacity prevents the Ghoul, log it explicitly, no refund.
- Refuse without action/cost if no fallen friendly summons and no capacity for the Ghoul. Otherwise once/fight use is consumed even if some fallen summons could not fit.
- **Black Pharaoh AOE trait:** every summoned ally's primary attack uses existing damage rules, then splashes **50% of actual primary HP damage (floor)** to **each other living enemy**, applying recipient mitigation once. No recursion, no extra on-hit status riders or kill/resource procs from splash. Primary damage override remains Wren-owned.
- Tests: no minions, all alive/cap full, two dead/one slot, ghoul fits/doesn't, oldest first, exact 50% HP, next-round timing, death ledger duplication, AOE 1 vs 3 enemies, serialization.

## N-2 Plague of Ages — `apply_max_infection_aoe` + `infections_true_damage`
- Preserve **once/fight, cost all Soul Energy (minimum 1)**; affects every living enemy, no positional radius. Refuse if there are no eligible living enemies. Apply/refresh **3 Infection stacks** to each eligible enemy via shared status pipeline, with standard **3-round** duration (**4** if Pestilent Sage applies). Do not bypass explicit immunity to receiving Infection.
- Infection applied/refreshed by this cast deals **true damage for that status instance's remaining lifetime**, not for the whole fight and not for unrelated DoTs. No immediate bonus tick. Base 3-stack tick = **6 true damage**; Harbinger adds **flat +2 per tick**, giving 8, not 12. True bypasses ordinary armor/resistance, not explicit true-damage immunity or status immunity.
- Normal Infection reapplication while the empowered status is active refreshes stacks/duration under ordinary rules but retains the true-damage flag until the status is removed or expires; cleanse removes it. Subsequent fresh ordinary Infection is normal damage unless Plague Sovereign passive applies.
- **Plague Sovereign passive:** all valid caster-owned DoT **damage** is true damage; it does **not** make targets susceptible to status types they are immune to, nor grant extra stacks/duration. Attribute DoT kills to the status source under existing rules; preserve Plague Reservoir rulings from PR #70.
- Tests: 0/1/2/3 prior stacks, immune target, selective cleanse, 3/4-turn duration, true damage against DEF, Harbinger flat addition, no immediate tick, source death, multi-target and once/fight.

## N-6 Eternal Lich / Unmake / Undying Will
- **Important source distinction:** `src/data.js` currently defines **Unmake only in Hollow King skills**, costing **5 Hollow Will**. Keep that Hollow King skill unchanged. Eternal Lich's description promises Unmake but the skill is not separately registered: **add a separate Eternal Lich access/skill definition**, with cost **5 Soul Energy**, no second resource. This is a new design authorization; do not globally rewrite the existing Hollow King skill.
- Eternal Lich Unmake: one living **non-boss** enemy; remove permanently **from the current fight**, not from world/save history. Bosses, explicit erase-immune targets and invalid/dead targets refuse without cost/action. No ordinary on-kill Soul Energy, corpses or Plague Reservoir for removal. May cast repeatedly if enough Soul Energy, unless its own branch data specifies a once/fight gate; this contract sets **once per fight** for Eternal Lich's variant to protect boss balance. Hollow King version remains unchanged.
- **Undying Will:** first otherwise lethal damage event in each fight is intercepted before final player death/defeat; restore **ceil(50% max HP)**, retain current Soul Energy, summons and debuffs; no extra action or rewind. Subsequent lethal event kills normally. Applies to enemy attacks, DoT and self-damage; does not trigger on explicit permanent erase.
- **Free Soul Feast:** once/fight, explicit selectable cast costs **0 Soul Energy instead of 2**; still requires live linked target(s), consumes normal action, uses all N-4 timing/refresh rules, and never generates a free link. Available regardless of whether Undying Will has triggered; counters are independent. Invalid free cast doesn't consume use.
- Tests: Hollow King Unmake remains 5 Hollow Will, Eternal Lich variant charges 5 Soul Energy only, 0/4/5 resource, boss immunity, kill rewards excluded, death 1/2, DoT/self-damage, 50% ceil, Feast without link/refusal, free Feast used once, fight reset, save/restore.
