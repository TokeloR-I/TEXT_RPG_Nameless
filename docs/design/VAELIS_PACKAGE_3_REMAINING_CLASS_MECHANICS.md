# Vaelis — Package 3: Remaining Class Mechanics
**Authority:** Creative Director delegated numeric gameplay decisions to Vaelis, 2026-10-23. **Status:** Approved gameplay contracts, code to be implemented separately. Data references checked against `src/data.js`. Preserve non-positional combat and single active resource (QA-8A).

## D-07 Warden Fortress — `create_zone`
- Preserve data-defined **2 Vigil cost, 3 normal caster turn opportunities, 20% incoming damage reduction**. Do not shorten to 2 turns.
- `Fortress` is a **side-wide defensive formation**, not a map coordinate: affects caster and all living friendly combatants (including summons) for the duration, even if summoned after casting. No spatial range, collision or movement checks. Enemies are never protected by the player's Fortress.
- Damage reduction applies to physical and magical HP damage **after flat DEF and before HP loss**, rounded **down** on the reduced damage; no reduction to true damage. For a 10-damage post-DEF hit, 20% reduction yields 8. It does not prevent statuses whose triggering hit otherwise landed.
- One Fortress per caster; recast **refreshes** its 3-turn duration, not stacks reduction. Two different allied casters' Fortresses use **the strongest single 20% reduction**, never 40%. Owner death or combat end clears the formation; holder death removes eligibility. Cleanse on an individual ally does not remove the caster-owned zone.
- Duration is measured by completed normal caster opportunities after casting, excluding the cast opportunity; stun/skipped normal opportunities count, bonus actions do not. Snapshot/restore must preserve owner, remaining duration and beneficiaries computed from current faction.
- Tests: cost/refusal, caster+minion+newly summoned ally, true damage, 10→8, two casters, recast, owner death, cleanse, skipped turn, save/load.

## D-08 Mage Overchannel — `overchannel`
- Source currently describes 'Spend HP to cast beyond Mana this turn; leaves a Burnout debuff next turn' but has no numeric effect. **Approved interpretation:** spend **4 HP to restore 3 Mana immediately** (capped at active resource maximum) as a **normal action**, and receive **Burnout on the next normal caster opportunity**: outgoing direct damage reduced **20% for that one opportunity**. Burnout does not change resource regeneration or DoT damage already applied.
- Why 3 Mana: existing free Focus restores 2 Mana, so Overchannel must be a higher-risk, higher-reward option rather than an objectively inferior HP-cost copy. This is a new approved numeric choice, not a previously shipped number.
- Overchannel is available only when the character's **active resource is Mana** (QA-8A); no hidden Mana restoration for evolved branches with Time/Deception or other resources. Refuse without action/HP cost if at Mana cap, if HP is **4 or less** (cannot self-kill with this skill), or if Burnout is already pending/active. Successful cast charges 0 Mana and 4 HP; if cap room is 1 or 2, grant only that much and still charge 4 HP (UI reports actual gain).
- Burnout attaches to caster via shared status pipeline, activates on the *next* normal opportunity, then expires after that opportunity even if skipped; bonus turns do not consume it. Cleanse removes it, not the HP cost. No second cast until Burnout expires; no stacking.
- Tests: 5 HP succeeds leaving 1, 4 HP refuses, Mana 0/near cap/at cap, evolved non-Mana refusal, Burnout next-turn 20%, stun/bonus turn, cleanse, snapshot, no free Focus equivalence.

## D-09 Illusionist Steal Skill — `steal_skill`
- Preserve existing **3 Deception Tokens**, enemy single target, **2 normal caster opportunities**. Copy **the targeted enemy's most recently completed active skill**, not an arbitrary ability. If enemy has used only a Basic Attack, that may be copied if the attack has a defined generic effect. No completed action or last skill is passive/ultimate/boss-exclusive/summon/permanent-erase/control-of-player → refuse without spending action/cost.
- The copied skill is added as **one temporary action**; copying never removes the enemy's own skill despite 'steal' flavor. Only one stolen skill slot; new successful cast replaces it. The copied action's **numeric cost** is paid from the Illusionist's current active resource (Deception Tokens), with original target legality and effect semantics; no second Mana/Energy pool. If original cost is 'all', it spends all current Deception Tokens, minimum 1. A copied action never gains access to another class's progression passives. A copy that cannot legally target anyone remains visible but disabled, not a free retarget.
- Duration: available on the next **two normal caster opportunities after the steal cast**, then removed. Using the copied skill consumes a normal action, not the slot (may be used again on the second opportunity if affordable). Stun/skipped normal opportunities count; bonus actions do not. Enemy death does not revoke a valid copy; caster death/combat end clears it. Copy does not recursively copy `steal_skill` or `rearrange_turn_order`.
- Tests: no enemy last skill, valid hit/miss completed action, target death, copied cost and insufficient funds, legal/illegal target, two opportunities, skipped, replacement, status expiry, serialization, UI/CLI action availability parity.

## D-09 Chronomancer Eternal Architect — `rearrange_turn_order`
- Preserve **cost all Time Charges**, **3 normal caster turn opportunities**, target all combatants. Requires at least **1 Time Charge**. Once activated, the player chooses a **permutation of all currently living combatants' normal turn slots** at the start of each of the next **3 rounds**; within each round every combatant still receives **exactly one normal opportunity**. No deleted/duplicated turns, no moving a completed opportunity backwards, and no altering bonus-action queue.
- Selection is a single player-authored order list through engine-owned action API, validated as a permutation of living entity IDs. Newly summoned combatants enter at the end of the selected order until next round's order choice; dead actors drop out. If the player provides no order at a round boundary, use the existing deterministic initiative order rather than freezing combat. At the end of the third affected round, normal initiative resumes.
- This ability **does not itself inflict stun, slow or full action denial**; it does not bypass Control Resistance and must not cause double DoT ticks. Bosses may be placed later in the same round but cannot be denied a turn. Recast refreshes the 3-round window and consumes all Time Charges, does not stack multiple reorder effects.
- If CLI/GUI cannot represent ordering selection, block deployment pending interface work; never silently pretend the effect works. Keel reviews round scheduler/serialization before implementation.
- Tests: 3 living actors permutation, duplicates/missing IDs rejected without cost for invalid activation input, boss still acts, deaths/summons, DoT once per actual turn, 3-round expiry, skipped opportunity, extra turn unchanged, save/load, UI/CLI parity.

## D-10 Assassin Shadowstep — `teleport_to_enemy`
- Preserve **1 Stealth Token** and existing `guaranteed_crit_next_hit` effect. In non-positional combat, `teleport_to_enemy` is **presentation-only** (dash/teleport animation and flavor event), not a range bypass, forced dodge, additional damage or physical position update. **Do not replace guaranteed critical with +25% crit chance.**
- Select a living enemy to anchor the animation; refuse if none exists. Apply guaranteed critical to the caster's **next successful direct damaging hit during this fight**; misses do not consume it, damage-only DoTs and minion hits do not consume it, and critical immunity still prevents a crit. Recast refreshes rather than stacks. Death or combat end clears it; cleanse of positive buffs removes it.
- Tests: cost, no enemy, animation event without position state, next direct hit crit, miss retains buff, DoT/minion doesn't consume, immunity, recast, fight reset.
