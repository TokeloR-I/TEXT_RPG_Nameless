# Soulbinder — N-3 / N-4 Gameplay Contracts
**Owner:** Vaelis, Lead Game Designer · **Date:** 2026-10-23
**Status:** DESIGN RESOLVED / developer handoff. No source implementation or numerical balance changes in this PR. Implements existing Soulbinder identity and stage descriptions. Rook owns implementation; Wren's work excluded.

## N-3 — Soul Chain and Chain of Woe
- **Cast:** costs 1 Soul Energy; select exactly two **distinct living enemies** at Soulbinder L3. At Chain of Woe L5, select exactly three distinct living enemies if available, otherwise two. Fewer than two valid enemies: refuse without cost or action. No friendly targets. Single active link group per caster; successful recast replaces prior group, failed recast does not.
- **Duration:** one **caster-owned link-group clock**, not independent per-target status clocks. Base two completed **caster normal turns after cast**; Binding Will adds one, making three. Count each subsequent normal caster turn at its end; the cast turn does not count. Member statuses are markers only and never decrement group duration. Stun/skip of the caster's normal turn still counts one turn opportunity; bonus actions do not. If caster dies the group clears immediately. This is a deliberate group-level lifecycle rule; do not reuse per-holder round ticking. Group expires when its duration ends, fight ends, owner dies, or fewer than two living members remain. Removing/cleansing one member drops only that member; if 2+ remain group continues. Do not propagate from dead or removed members.
- **Trigger:** Any positive *actual HP damage* to one linked enemy from a direct attack, skill, DoT or environmental source triggers one secondary hit on **each other living linked enemy at the moment of the primary hit**. Snapshot recipients before primary damage resolves; apply primary damage and its death processing first, then apply snapshotted secondary hits even if primary death dissolved the group. Recipients who themselves died or became invalid during primary resolution receive no secondary hit. Healing, prevented damage, overkill beyond target's remaining HP, status application without HP loss and zero damage do not trigger.
- **Amount:** each secondary hit starts at **floor(actual HP loss on primary × 0.5)**; each secondary receives the full value (no splitting across group). A 0 result does nothing.
- **Damage classification:** secondary is **magical, noncritical, secondary link damage**, not a fresh cast or attack. It **bypasses flat DEF** and does not gain caster stat scaling, crit, on-hit status riders, lifesteal or kill-trigger bonuses beyond ordinary kill ownership rules. Apply the recipient's applicable damage-reduction, immunity and prevention mechanics **once**, using existing combat pipeline. Do not reapply the primary target's defenses. Preserve any status-specific immunity that blocks magical damage. No Soul Chain propagation from secondary damage, even if it kills; guard recursion by origin metadata.
- **Wound Transfer:** L5 linked enemies take +10% incoming damage via existing incoming-damage modifiers, including on original direct hit and secondary damage where eligible. To prevent multiplicative unintended growth, calculate primary actual HP loss after modifiers; derive 50%; apply secondary's own applicable +10% once and mitigation once. Do not apply Wound Transfer twice.
- **Ownership:** secondary link damage is attributed to the Soulbinder caster for combat logging only; **no on-kill gameplay rewards or kill-trigger passives** arise from link, Feast or Spirit Lash damage (see K3 below). On owner death clear link group; fight cleanup removes all link data. Links do not cross fights.
- **No stacking:** link groups from same caster never overlap. For multiple Soulbinders, each actor's group is independently owned; secondary damage is never eligible to trigger *any* link propagation.

## N-4 — Soul Feast, Spirit Lash and Eidolon King
- **Cast:** costs 2 Soul Energy; requires at least one living enemy currently linked by caster. Refuse for free if none. Applying Feast creates one **3-tick** active drain effect owned by caster; recasting refreshes to 3 ticks and does not stack or cause an immediate bonus tick.
- **Timing:** drain occurs **at start of each affected linked enemy's normal turn opportunity, including stunned/skipped turns** using the common status/DoT pipeline. Each eligible enemy can be drained at most once per turn; Haste/extra actions do not generate additional ticks. Duration is three eligible target-turn opportunities after cast, tracked per target. If target leaves group, dies, is cleansed or combat ends, stop draining it; newly linked enemies after Feast cast do not automatically join that existing Feast. A successful recast establishes a new snapshot of currently linked targets and refreshes their counters.
- **Base amount:** 2 magical drain damage per target per tick. It **bypasses flat DEF** but respects damage immunity, reduction and prevention applicable to magical effects. Damage is capped by target's remaining HP. Drain does not crit, receive stat scaling or apply on-hit riders. It **never triggers Soul Chain secondary damage**, nor is it triggered by it.
- **Healing:** base Soul Feast heals **0**. With Eidolon King, heal caster for **100% of actual HP removed by Feast** after mitigation, capped at max HP. No healing on immune/prevented damage, no overkill healing. The upgrade does not modify Feast's 2 damage or 3-tick duration.
- **Spirit Lash:** separate L8 passive: each living linked enemy takes 3 damage once per normal turn opportunity (including stunned/skipped turns) while linked; apply standard damage prevention and attribution, no recursive link propagation, no Soul Feast healing from Spirit Lash.
- **Expiration:** Feast effects end after three target opportunities, if caster dies, on combat end, or when target is no longer linked. A new link group does not transfer old Feast instances. Do not apply old Feast ticks to newly linked targets.
- **Cleanse:** removing Soul Chain link from an enemy also removes its Feast participation. A generic DoT cleanse removes Feast participation from the cleansed enemy but does not necessarily break Soul Chain unless the cleanse also targets link status.

## Keel K1–K3 final architecture rulings (2026-10-23)
These override any earlier phrasing in this contract that could imply per-holder link duration or ordinary attack-trigger behavior.

- **K1 — Group clock:** Soul Chain uses **one caster-owned counter**, decremented once after each subsequent normal caster turn opportunity (including stun/skip); cast turn excluded, bonus turns excluded. Member markers have no independent duration. Thus faster/slower enemies cannot cause different link expiration. This requires a small group-lifecycle mechanism rather than assuming the current per-holder status tick implements it.
- **K2 — Lethal propagation:** snapshot living secondary recipients **before** the primary damage call; resolve primary HP loss/death, then resolve snapshot secondaries. If primary death dissolved the group, these already-earned secondary hits **still occur**; never retarget, and skip any snapshotted recipient now dead/removed. Propagation remains nonrecursive.
- **K3 — Damage side effects:** classify `link`, `drain`, and `aura` as **secondary effect damage**, not a basic/skill hit. All three may reduce HP and cause normal death and victory, but **do not** (a) satisfy the Necromancer's end-round “player dealt damage” +1 Soul Energy eligibility, (b) grant +2 Soul Energy for a kill or trigger Plague Reservoir/other `onKill` resource/passive rewards, (c) receive Expose or Synergy Marks amplification, or (d) consume Marked for Death or other positive-hit marks. They do not apply weapon/on-hit effects, create additional link propagation or trigger ordinary lifesteal. Feast's explicit Eidolon King healing remains its only healing exception. Original *primary* direct hits still process all their ordinary rewards/marks. These exclusions intentionally avoid changing the resource economy being measured in #40; reassess only with new balance evidence.
- **Skipped turns:** Soul Feast and Spirit Lash tick once at the beginning of each eligible enemy's **normal turn opportunity even when stunned**, consistent with existing DoT behavior. Bonus actions never add ticks.

**Deterministic additions:** (11) two linked enemies with different speeds keep membership through exactly the same two/three caster opportunities; (12) lethal primary on A still deals its snapshotted half-damage to B; (13) secondary link/Feast/Lash kills never grant +2 Soul Energy or onKill, and secondary damage never grants end-round +1 Soul Energy; (14) secondary damage does not amplify through Expose/Synergy or consume Marked for Death; (15) stunned enemy receives Feast/Lash tick once, extra action none; (16) primary direct damage continues to trigger its ordinary marks and resource effects independently.

## Fixed-seed deterministic acceptance cases
1. Base Soul Chain cast with two targets costs 1, duration 2; Binding Will makes duration 3; Chain of Woe capacity 3; invalid targets refuse without spending turn/resource.
2. Original attack causes 11 **actual HP loss** to A; B receives floor(11×.5)=5 **before B's reduction**; DEF alone cannot reduce the 5. At 3 linked targets, B and C each receive 5.
3. A's damage prevented: 0 propagation. A has 3 HP but would take 20: propagation starts from 3 actual HP loss, so each other gets 1 before reductions.
4. B has 20% applicable magical damage reduction: 5 propagated becomes 4; an applicable magical immunity results in 0. Secondary cannot crit or recursively propagate.
5. Wound Transfer applies +10% once to primary and secondary; no repeated stacking. Verify rounding under current damage pipeline.
6. Remove one member from a three-target group: two remain linked. Remove another: group clears. Owner death and fight cleanup clear all links.
7. Soul Feast 2 HP per eligible linked target at start of their next three normal turns; no immediate cast tick; no double ticks from extra actions. Base heals 0; Eidolon King heals exactly actual damage capped at caster missing HP.
8. Recast Feast refreshes instead of stacking; re-linking without re-casting Feast does not transfer the old drain. Cleanse and death prevent further ticks.
9. Spirit Lash does not count as Feast damage and cannot heal Eidolon King; Feast and Spirit Lash cannot recursively trigger links.
10. Fixed seed with simultaneous DoT death, link expiry and enemy turn order produces identical results across runs; old CLI and combat tests pass.
