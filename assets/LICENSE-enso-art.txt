ENSO original art — made for and owned by the Enso's Last Stand project (Tokelo).

assets/sprites/enso-player.js (the player and villager character) and ui/scene-art.js (the homestead grounds and
other scenes) are drawn entirely by code: every frame is built
from shapes (ellipses, limbs, polygons) on a pose skeleton and coloured from our own ramps. No image, art pack,
screenshot or generator output was used as input or reference. In particular no Mana Seed art
(docs/art/history/LICENSE-manaseed.txt) and nothing from assets/basesprite/ was used.
