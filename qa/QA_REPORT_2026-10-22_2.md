## Design gaps
1. **Riposte vs an absorbed hit:** a dodged attack triggers Riposte; an absorbed one doesn't. Should "counter the next melee attack" fire when Bone Wall soaks it?
2. **What does "absorbs the next hit entirely" cover?** Damage only (today), or also the attack's riders: stun, poison, marks (QA-14)? The same question applies to dodges and Blind misses.
3. **Zero-damage hits use up the wall.** A Drunken Shade "Disorienting Whisper" (1–3 vs your DEF) can consume the wall. PR #17 does this deliberately. Confirm it's intended.
4. **Soul Energy economy for defensive casts:** should a turn spent on Bone Wall (or any non-damage spell) still earn the per-round Soul Energy? Today it doesn't, which is the main reason the skill is dead at L3.
