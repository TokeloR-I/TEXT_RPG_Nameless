## Summary
1. **One inspect can finish the whole chapter (QA-41, new, blocker).**
   - The route: leave the cellar without reading the inscription, walk to the road, come back and look at the foundation stone.
   - That single inspect completes **Q01 through Q08**. The Warden, the seal and Chapter One are all marked done, with no fight.
   - You get `fragment_one`, `aspect_nahemoth` (Black Flight) and `chapter_one_complete`, at level 2.
   - Cause: Wren's new `catchUpChain` (7b01d66) overshoots its target. Details in the table.
   - The fuzz runs hit this in 7 of 40 games. That is also why the fuzz runs showed 84 Black Flights from players who had never reached the orchard.
2. **The report #11 fixes hold.**
   - 24/24 NPCs and enemies block their whole 2×2 block and answer from every side.
   - All four gate fights hold from both directions.
   - The Captain now names what is missing.
3. **Chapter One still cannot be finished (QA-32, still open, blocker).**
   - `build` is on `main` now, but `constructionDefinitions` is `{}`. So Orin's gate answers "This construction is unavailable", and Q07 never completes.
4. **You can't buy anything in the browser (QA-42, new, blocker for the shops).**
   - Merchants list their stock, then say *"To buy: 'buy mara <item>'"*. But `world.html` has no text input, and no buy or build action in its talk or menu UI.
   - So all four shops are window displays in the version Tokelo plays.
5. **Gold is about a fifth of the design (QA-43, major).**
   - The only Gold source is 2 per kill.
   - On the mandatory path you have **14 Gold before the Warden and 16 after**. The fixture wants 78 before, +70 guaranteed after, and a 60 Gold gate.
   - With every optional fight and Shane's quest you have 26.
   - So you can buy about one Bandages roll before the Warden.
6. **Shops work from anywhere (QA-44, new, major).**
   - `buy iona Bandages` works while you stand in the cellar.
   - `act({type:'buy', shopId:'shane'})` works before Shane is even recruited.
7. **The Warden is still the wall, and B-01/B-02 is not on `main` (QA-45, balance, major).**
   - Shipped Warden win rates: Warrior **55%**, Mage **15%**, Rogue **28%**, Necromancer **0%**.
   - Vaelis's approved B-01 (branch resource regen) and B-02 (+1 DEF) are not implemented.
   - Applying them as written (`--set`): Mage 44%, Rogue **86%**, Necromancer **1%**, Warrior 55%. So the Rogue passes the Warrior by 31 points, and the Necromancer is not helped at all.
   - This needs Vaelis before Wren ships it.
8. **Minor issues.**
   - `buy` is case-sensitive (QA-46).
   - The starting inventory has no item definitions (QA-47).
   - Two inspect spots can't be reached on the 16 px maps (QA-48).
   - The text build still needs the Tailwind CDN (QA-49).
9. **Trees now look cropped (QA-50, new, major visual).** The QA-35 fix keeps the paths clear, but trees are now just a trunk in a dark square.

## Balance: Warden win % (100 seeds per class, real route, 95% CI)
G1 to G4 are 100% for every class in every variant. The Warden is the only fight that loses.

| Variant | Warrior | Mage | Rogue | Necromancer |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Shipped, smart bot | 55 (45–64) | 15 (9–23) | 28 (20–37) | 0 (0–4) |
| Shipped, simple bot | 59 (49–68) | 18 (12–27) | 18 (12–27) | 0 (0–4) |
| `--buy` (all Gold on healing after Q05) | 71 (61–79) | 19 (13–28) | 34 (25–44) | 2 (1–7) |
| `--buy --pickups` | 95 (89–98) | 35 (26–45) | 64 (54–73) | 41 (32–51) |
| B-01 + B-02 as approved | 55 (45–64) | 44 (35–54) | **86 (78–91)** | 1 (0–5) |

The B-01 + B-02 run used:
```
--set "resourceRules.Stealth Tokens.regen=1" --set "resourceRules.Combo Points.regen=1" --set "resourceRules.Deception Tokens.regen=2"
--set "basePlayerClasses.mage.baseStats.def=3" --set "basePlayerClasses.rogue.baseStats.def=4" --set "basePlayerClasses.necromancer.baseStats.def=5"
```

Reading the numbers:
- Shopping helps the Warrior most, because Gold buys one or two heals at most.
- Healing items are the biggest single lever: with pickups as well, the Necromancer goes from 0% to 41%. A working shop with an economy near the fixture would likely move every class more than B-01/B-02 does.
- **Text dungeon** (`npm run balance`, 60 runs): Warrior 53%, Mage **0%** (51 deaths in the cellar tunnels), Rogue 10%, Necromancer 3%.

**XP curve.**
- Main quests pay 15/20/25/25/20/30 XP for Q01 to Q06, 135 in all. B01, Q07 and Q08 pay 0.
- Levels come at 100, 250 and 475 total XP (each level ×1.5).
- On the real route every class reaches L2 at G3 and L3 (branch choice) just before the Warden. That's the design, and it holds now that the gates hold.
- QA-41 breaks it: the cascade pays 135 quest XP at once and leaves you at L2 with Chapter One "complete".
