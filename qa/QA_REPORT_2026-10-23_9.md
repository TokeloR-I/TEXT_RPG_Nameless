### #81 Possession — PASS (`possession.js` 14/14)
- **Learning and cost:** real L8 Wraithlord. The cast costs 3 SE and the turn, and adds +35 CR.
- **Refused for free:** boss, explicit immunity, CR 75, and only 2 SE. CR 74 is allowed. Availability and `act()` give the same `REQUIREMENT_FAILED` message for the boss.
- **The controlled turn:**
  - It pauses for your choice whether the enemy is slower or **faster** than you; the control survives the round end.
  - Targets are its former allies only: never itself, never you.
  - Initiative is unchanged, it acts once, and nobody gets an extra turn.
- **When control ends:**
  - A stunned holder loses the turn *and* the control.
  - Cleanse or death end the control.
  - With no legal move, the engine does the no-op itself and doesn't pause.
- **Kill credit:** a possession kill gives no Soul Energy and no player kill credit.
- **Refusals while waiting:** anything else is `WRONG_MODE`, and a self-target is `INVALID_TARGET`. State is unchanged.
- **Parity:** CLI and `act()` give identical state.
- **Snapshot:** taken while waiting for control and restored into a fresh engine, it is still waiting and gives the same result.
- **Recast:** casting on a new enemy releases the first.
- **INFO for Vaelis:** Possession on a **lone** non-boss enemy is a guaranteed lost turn for 3 SE. That's the contract's no-op, and with +35 CR it behaves like a stun.
- **Merge:** a docs-only conflict with `main` in `architecture/PHASE1_ENGINE_INTERFACE_SPEC.md` (Keel's v0.2.1 landed in #74). Rebase needed.

### #82 Eternal Architect — PASS (`turn_order.js` 13/13)
- **Learning and cost:** real L14 Eternal Architect. 0 Time Charges is refused free; the cast spends all charges.
- **Window:** pauses at the start of each of the next 3 rounds, then normal initiative resumes.
- **Order applied:** you last in round N and first in N+1 works, and everyone acts exactly once per round.
- **Bad orders:** duplicate, missing and unknown ids are `INVALID_ORDER` at no cost, and the round keeps waiting. A skill while waiting is `WRONG_MODE`.
- **Recast** refreshes to 3 and doesn't stack (5 pauses in total, not 6).
- **The boss** gets one turn per window round wherever it's placed.
- **DoTs** tick once per actual turn.
- **A stunned enemy** is skipped once, in its slot. A dead enemy drops out of the next choice.
- **Haste** adds no normal turn.
- **Parity:** CLI positions and `act()` ids give identical state.
- **Snapshot** while waiting → fresh engine works.
- **Flee** ends the window.
- **Note:** you can't flee or act while the order is pending (`WRONG_MODE`). Keel §3.2 asks for an explicit "keep the order" answer, so the engine waits rather than "using the default". That's fine, just worth knowing for Sable.
- **Merge:** same docs-only conflict as #81.

### #77 Eternal Lich — 5/8, not ready
- **Unmake:** the real L14 Eternal Lich gets "Eternal Lich Unmake" for 5 Soul Energy, once per fight; Hollow King Unmake stays at 5 Hollow Will. With 4 SE, or against the boss, it is refused at no cost. Erasing gives no SE, no corpse and no kill credit.
- **Undying Will:** an enemy hit and Channel the Grave self-damage at 1 HP each revive you at ceil(50%) = 5/10. Soul Energy, minions and debuffs are kept, and the second lethal hit kills.
- **Free Soul Feast:**
  - The first cast is free and the next one costs 2.
  - A refused cast (no link) doesn't use up the free cast.
  - It's independent of Undying Will and resets each fight.
- **Snapshot:** a JSON snapshot restored into a fresh engine keeps both flags.
- **QA-29 (major, latent): Undying Will revives you after your "death" has already been processed.**
  - The interception sits in `checkBattleEnd()`, but `damage()` has already run `onDeath(player)`.
  - That strips the caster's **Soul Chain and Soul Feast** from every enemy: 3 links and 3 Feasts before, 0 and 0 after the revive. It also orphans your applier-death marks.
  - The contract says the lethal event is "intercepted before final player death", and N-3 clears links only "if the caster dies".
  - The Eternal Lich is a Soulbinder, so its own L14 passive wipes its own links.
- **QA-30 (major, latent): Undying Will after a DoT death at the start of your turn.**
  - `startTurn()` sees HP 0, so it doesn't arm your statuses and it skips your turn.
  - After the revive, the killing **1-round Poison is still unarmed**. It survives the round end and **ticks again** at your next turn.
  - A 50-damage, 1-round Poison therefore kills you, Undying Will revives you, and the same Poison kills you again. One Guard input goes all the way to DEFEAT, and the revived turn is lost too.
  - **Suggested fix for both:** intercept the lethal event inside `damage()`, before `onDeath()`. That is also what Keel's "one lethal-interception point" implies.
- **QA-31 (cosmetic):** typing `unmake 1` as an Eternal Lich answers "You don't know the skill 'Unmake'". The skill is named "Eternal Lich Unmake", so it needs an alias or a shorter name.

### #78 March of Endless Bones / Black Pharaoh — 6/6
- **Learning and refusals:** real L14 Black Pharaoh, cap 6. Full cap with nobody fallen is refused free, and 0 SE is refused.
- **Resurrection:**
  - Oldest death first, with new ids.
  - A 21-HP skeleton returns at ceil(50%) = 11, with no carried statuses, and doesn't act this round.
  - The Ghoul is added if a slot remains.
- **One slot, two fallen:** only the oldest returns, the log says "No capacity remains for the free Ghoul.", and a second March is `ONCE_PER_FIGHT`.
- **Splash:**
  - An 11-damage skeleton hit splashes 5 / 5 to the others, and nothing with one enemy.
  - The Ghoul's Weaken isn't applied to splash targets.
  - A splash kill pays no Soul Energy.
- **Snapshot:** the death ledger survives a JSON snapshot into a fresh engine.
- **Design question (Vaelis):** "recipient mitigation once" for the splash means a DEF 3 recipient still takes the full 5. Splash uses the Soul Chain path, which bypasses flat DEF. Is DEF "recipient mitigation" here? It isn't for Soul Chain.
- **Merge:** conflicts with #75/#76/#77 (adjacent `register` blocks in `effects.js`) and with Wren's stack (the `startCombat` field list in `combat.js`). Both are additive, but a rebase is needed.
