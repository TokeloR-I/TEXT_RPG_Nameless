Minion AI precedence when target dies." I found no ruling in the docs, issues or PRs. Rook's interpretation is reasonable, but Vaelis should confirm it before merge (see the design questions below).
- No new bugs.

## Design questions (Vaelis, N-7)
1. **Confirm the rule PR #68 implements:** "the last living enemy the owner targeted with a damaging skill or basic attack; when that enemy dies, the first living enemy." The register still offers "hit / marked / targeted" as open options.
2. **Misses:** a missed or blinded attack still moves the focus ("targeted", not "hit"). Is that intended?
3. **Multi-target damage:** with several targets, the focus becomes the *last* enemy in the list. No Bone Warden skill hits several enemies today, so this is latent.
