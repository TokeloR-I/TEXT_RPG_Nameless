# QA Report 2026-10-25 #13: Warden balance on the new loot, Gold and starter gear (on `main` 38f2aaf)

*Tally, QA & Balance Tester.* This is the sign-off measurement PR #172 asks for: its own numbers are developer measurements, not a QA sign-off.

**Build.** `main` at 38f2aaf. It has:
- #163, #164 and #165: Wren's fixes for report #12.
- #171: the merchant UI.
- #172: universal victory loot, once-only quest and seal Gold, and auto-equipped starter gear.

`npm test`: **403/403 pass.**

**What #172 does and does not change.**
- **Changed:** loot, Gold and starter gear.
- **Not changed:** the class stats. The class candidates in `docs/design/ENSO_CH1_QA12_NUMERIC_DECISIONS_WREN_HANDOFF.md` (#167) are not applied: Mage 70 HP/2 DEF and Necromancer 80 HP/4 DEF are as before.
- So I measured shipped `main`, then each candidate on top of it through `--set`.

**Method.** `qa/scripts/ch1_survival.js` plays the real Chapter One route with real combat: G1 to G4, the L3 branch choice, then the Warden.
- **Setup:** 200 matched seeds per class, smart bot unless noted.
- **Shopping (`--buy`):** after Q05, talk to Iona and Mara and spend all Gold on healing.
- **What the shopping runs leave out:** the cellar purse (10 Gold) and optional fights, so the Gold figures are conservative.
- **Script fix:** buying now needs the merchant's stock open (QA-44 fix), so `--buy` talks to each merchant first. Before this fix it silently bought nothing on the new `main`.

## Summary
1. **Shopping is now the whole story.** Gold reaches about 45 at the Shamefield shops, enough for about 4 heals, and the Warden goes from a wall to mostly beatable.

   | Warden win % (95% CI) | Warrior | Mage | Rogue | Necromancer |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   | `main`, no shopping | 89 (83–92) | 21 (16–27) | 47 (40–54) | 15 (10–20) |
   | **`main`, shopping** | **100** (98–100) | **74** (68–80) | **87** (82–91) | **98** (94–99) |
   | `main`, shopping, simple bot | 100 | 66 | 83 | 98 |

   Report #12 (old economy, no shopping) had W 55 · M 15 · R 28 · N 0.

2. **Don't ship the #167 class tweaks as they are (QA-51).** With shopping they push every class to 92–100%: W 100 · M 96 · R 92 · N 100. That breaks Vaelis's own "no class over 90%" guide, and without the tweaks the Mage is already at 74 and the Necromancer at 98.
3. **Only one branch misses the 60% target: Chronomancer, at 46% (QA-52).** Assassin is close at 61%. Every other branch is at 78% or more.
   - Warrior (100%) and Necromancer (98%) are over the 90% ceiling. That's a call for Vaelis: is the Warden meant to be this easy once you shop?
4. **The single B-01 regens, each tested alone on top of shopping:**
   - Stealth +1 takes the Assassin from 61% to **100%**, and the Rogue to 100% overall. That's too much.
   - Combo +1 changes nothing, because the Duelist is already at 100%.
   - Deception +2 takes the Illusionist from 78% to 100%.
   - None of them helps the Chronomancer.
5. **No softlocks and no Gold or XP dupes found.**
   - The fuzzer ran 40 games × 6000 steps with 479 save/loads: no broken invariant and no exception. No quest-chain cascade, and no early Black Flight.
   - Loot is paid once and only on victory: fleeing pays nothing, and re-entering or reloading pays nothing more.
   - Gold on the mandatory route with the purse: 74 before the Warden, 94 after the seal.
   - All 800 shopping runs reach the Warden at exactly 250 XP and L3.
