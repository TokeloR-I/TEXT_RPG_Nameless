## Design questions (PR #52)
1. **Bone Wall vs Feast.** A Feast tick is absorbed by (and uses up) Bone Wall on a linked enemy, while a Poison tick isn't, because Bone Wall's rule since PR #17 is "DoTs aren't hits". The contract builds Feast on the DoT pipeline, so should Bone Wall absorb it? (Bone Wall absorbing a secondary *link* hit matches the contract's "prevention" wording.)
2. **Feast's 3 ticks don't fit inside a Soul Chain.** In real play (link, then Feast the next turn) only **2** ticks land before the link ends, in both speed orders, and under K1's caster clock as well. Feast only reaches its 3 ticks if the link is recast. Is that intended?
