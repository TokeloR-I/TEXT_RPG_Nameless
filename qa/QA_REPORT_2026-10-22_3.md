## QA-14 regression sweep (`node qa/scripts/riders.js`, 12/12 pass)
The fix filters **every** `apply` effect of a damaging skill to the targets the attack reached. That's only safe if no such effect is meant to land anyway, so each one was checked:

| Damaging skill | Filtered effect | Kind | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hemorrhage, Shield Bash, Garrote, Reaper's Charge, Ice Lance | bleed, stun, silence, stun, slow | on-target rider | still applied on a connecting hit, including a 0-damage hit through armor |
| Lunge, Mounted Charge | gain_resource | self effect, ignores targets | still grants +2 Fury / +1 Oath Points when the target dodges |
| Corpse Bloom | aoe_damage_from_corpse, apply_infection_aoe | combat-level cost / per-target rider | corpse still consumed; infection only on enemies it reached |
| Ghoul minion (Raise Ghoul) | Weaken mark | on-target rider (summon) | still applied through the minion's attack |

Also checked: in a multi-hit attack where hit 1 is absorbed and hit 2 connects, the rider **does** land. An area attack applies its rider to each enemy it reached and not to the one that dodged. Mutation check: removing the filter fails 3 of the new QA-14 tests (108/111).

**For new content:** a future damaging skill whose `apply` effect is meant to happen even on a miss (a self-buff that reads `ctx.targets`, say) would be silently skipped by this filter. Today none exists; worth one line in the effects.js hook docs.
