# QA Report 2026-10-24 #10 — Chapter One: class survival per encounter (on `main` 71a6532)

*Tally — QA & Balance Tester.*
- **Wren's hand-off** (`docs/CHAPTER_ONE.md`, "Hand-offs"): "optional XP (S01 20, J01 10, J02 10) and the constable kit are marked `candidate`. The mainline trace is in the tests; class survival per encounter is yours."
- **The plan this follows:** Vaelis's test plan in `docs/design/ENSO_CH1_ENCOUNTERS_XP_AND_BALANCE.md` ("Explicit Tally balance/QA assignment").

**Method.**
- `qa/scripts/ch1_survival.js` plays the **real world route with real combat**: no forced HP and no cheats. The chapter test forces enemy HP to 1 (PR #94 points this out), so it doesn't measure survival.
- **Route:** every required fight (G1–G4), then the level-3 branch choice and the Warden (B01).
- **HP carries between fights,** as in the game. Level-ups heal you to full: L2 arrives right after G2, and L3 right before the Warden.
- **Sample:** 200 seeds per class, with two bots. The *smart* bot is the QA bot (expected-damage choice, heals below 45%). The *simple* bot is the balance tool's (biggest affordable skill on the weakest enemy).
- **Branches:** each branch takes a third of the Warden runs (67/67/66).
- **Optional fights** (rats, wolf, Shane's constables) are measured alone, at the level you meet them.
- **Healing:** "Pickups" means the three healing items lying on the route: shed Bandages, well bottle, orchard herbs.
- **Variants:** the approved but **not yet implemented** #70 decisions are tested on scratch data only, separately and combined: B-01 regen (Stealth 1, Combo 1, Deception 2) and B-02 (+1 DEF for Mage, Rogue and Necromancer).

## Summary
1. **The XP guarantee holds in real play.** 800/800 smart-bot runs reached the Warden at exactly **250 XP and level 3**, with every optional thing skipped. The simple bot gives the same result.
2. **The four required fights are safe for every class:** **100% wins** for both bots, all classes. The tightest is Mage G4: on average 45 of 80 HP left, and as low as 23.
3. **The Warden is the wall.** You meet him at full HP, freshly L3. Wins, smart bot, as shipped: **Warrior 55% · Rogue 31% · Mage 17% · Necromancer 0%.** He wins every lost run.
   - It depends heavily on the branch: Berserker 99% against Warden 10%; Elementalist 52% against Illusionist and Chronomancer 0%; Saboteur 82% against Assassin 0%.
   - No Necromancer branch wins at all.
4. **The approved but unimplemented #70 changes matter a lot here.** With B-01 and B-02 together: Rogue 31% → **88%**, Mage 17% → **43%**, but Necromancer still **2%**. Add the three route pickups: Warrior 91%, Rogue 100%, Mage 63%, Necromancer 57%.
5. **Healing is the lever.** No shop has stock and there's no Gold, so the only healing on the mainline is the level-up heal plus three optional pickups. Those pickups alone take Warrior 55% → 91% and Necromancer 0% → 23%.
6. **The optional fights are trivial:** 100% everywhere. The candidate constables barely scratch anyone (biggest hit 4–7; Warrior, Rogue and Necromancer lose nothing on average), so S01 pays **50 XP** (20 + 2×15) at no risk. Doing everything optional reaches L3 just before G3 (at J02), with 355 XP at the Warden.
7. **QA-32 (blocker for finishing the chapter): Rook's PR #91 never reached `main`.** It was merged into the #90 branch 17 seconds *after* #90 had landed on `main`.
   - Its 7 commits (`build` / farming / crafting) are stranded, and `build homestead_gate` is an unknown command on `main`.
   - Even with #91 merged locally, the build is "unavailable": there's no `constructionDefinitions.homestead_gate`, no Gold and no Gold source.
   - So **Q07, and with it Chapter One, can't be finished in real play** (design gap G-1). The chapter test passes because it calls `storyEvent({ type: 'build' })` directly.

**Reading this** (measurements, not recommendations):
- **XP adequacy and combat difficulty are separate problems.** The XP route is fine. The difficulty is concentrated in one fight, the Warden, which you meet at full HP with whatever healing you happened to find.
- **Necromancer can't beat the Warden as shipped, with any branch or bot.** B-02 alone doesn't change that (2%). Only healing items move it (23%; 57% combined). That matches the #40 evidence (Soul Energy income, Bone Wall's opportunity cost), which #70 B-03 left for a separate defensive redesign.
- **Chronomancer is at 0–7% in every variant.** At L3 it only has Haste, and both bots use Time Charges poorly. A human may do better, so treat this as a lower bound, as in report #5.
- **The Warrior's Warden branch (10%)** is the weakest Warrior branch by far. Every other Warrior branch is at 55% or better.

## Design gaps (Vaelis / Rook; not bugs)
- **G-1: Q07 can't be completed without economy data.** Even with #91, `homestead_gate` has no construction definition. The player also has 0 Gold, with no Gold source and no shop stock (`shopDefinitions` is `{}`). So the chapter's final quest and the Q08 hook can't be reached in play. This waits on D-5 (starter Gold, guaranteed sources, first repair fee). The design's stop rule says the "first repair remains achievable without random money drops".
- **G-2: No healing on the mainline except level-ups.** The merchants Mara, Bragg, Iona and Shane have nothing to sell. Healing is the biggest single lever on Warden survival (§2), so the economy decisions are also balance decisions for the Warden.
- **G-3: The candidate constable kit is a pushover:** 100% wins, at most a 4–7 hit, and most classes lose no HP. S01's 50 XP is risk-free; the wolf, at 15 XP, is more dangerous. Measured, not decided: CH1-06 is Rook's and the numbers are Vaelis's.

## For the decision sheet (#40 / #70 follow-up)
- **B-01 and B-02 are approved but not yet in the code.** Implementing them is the largest measured improvement for Rogue (31 → 88%) and Mage (17 → 43%) at the Chapter One Warden. They do nothing for Necromancer.
- **Necromancer needs its own answer before Chapter One ships:** 0% as shipped, 2% with B-01/B-02.
