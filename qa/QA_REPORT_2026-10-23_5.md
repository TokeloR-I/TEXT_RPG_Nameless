# QA Report 2026-10-23 #5 — commit 250a77b · balance evidence lane (#29 + #40)

*Tally — QA & Balance Tester.* Routed by the design blocker audit (`docs/design/DESIGN_BLOCKER_AUDIT_2026-10-23.md`, Q-01–Q-03).
One evidence lane for **#29** (QA-8B isolated regeneration) and **#40** (P1 balance recovery). **Measurement only:** scratch data copies and `--set`; no change to `src/`, `tests/` or game data. Nothing here is approved: 8A is approved, while 8B rates, DEF changes and Soul Energy triggers are **not**. Every value tested below is a scratch hypothesis.

## Summary
- **Build:** `main` 250a77b, no code change since report #4. `npm test` 139/139. The harness only observes the engine; instrumented and uninstrumented runs give identical outcomes (`--selfcheck`).
- **Regen (#29):** each candidate rate moves only its own branch. **The starvation problem is zero regen, not too little:** a rate of **1** removes starvation for Assassin and Duelist with no waste, while the candidate 3 wastes 24–30% of income at the cap. **Time Charges 2 and Stealth 2–3 create control loops at level 5**: a Chronomancer can deny 100% of the boss's turns with `slow`, and an Assassin can keep the boss silenced 91% of the time, because CR is still off.
- **DEF (#40):** DEF is the only lever that moves **full runs** for Mage and Necromancer. Regen alone leaves Mage at 0–2%, because 99% of Mage runs die before level 3. **+2 DEF:** mage 26%, rogue 61%, necro 60% (shipped tool). **+1 DEF:** 14%, 31%, 6%. Warrior stays at 56% in every variant.
- **Necromancer (#40):** Bone Wall has the **double cost** at level 3: a wall turn spends 1 Soul Energy *and* forfeits the +1 that only your own damage earns (18.3 vs 3.0 forfeited per boss fight). Every wall rotation **loses the boss fight 100% of the time** (direct damage wins 30%), and walls don't even save HP overall (183 taken vs 175), because the fight runs 10–14 rounds longer. **Any unconditional +1 Soul Energy** makes Bone Wall worth casting (every-other-round rotation 0 → 39%), **but also creates a turtle loop**: a level-8 Gravekeeper walls 47 rounds in a row and takes 6 damage in the whole fight while its ghoul wins.

## Method
- **Seeds:** 1–100 per cell (factorial and sensitivity); 1–200 for the Necromancer rotations and exploit check. Every variant uses the same seeds, bots and progression. Win % is shown with 95% Wilson intervals where it matters: at n=100 a cell near 50% is ±10 points, so differences of a few points are noise.
- **Scenarios:**
  - *Boss alone* is report #1's setup: level 3 in the branch, full HP, Bandages + Half-full Wine + Healing Herb (77 HP of healing), straight into the Warden. It isolates the branch resource.
  - *Full dungeon* is the real run with no cheats, the same path as `tools/balance.js`.
- **Bots:**
  - *smart*: `qa/scripts/smartbot.js`, picks by expected damage, summons, casts Haste first.
  - *simple*: the shipped `tools/balance.js` policy, the biggest affordable damaging skill on the weakest enemy.
  - Both are shown because they disagree exactly where a skill's value is a judgement call.
- **Variants** (scratch): **B** shipped · **R:** one candidate regen at a time (Stealth 3, Combo 3, Deception 2, Time 2) · **R4** all four · **D+1 / D+2** flat DEF for Mage, Rogue and Necromancer only · **R4+D1 / R4+D2**.
- **Reproduce** (deterministic):
  ```
  node qa/scripts/balance_lane.js 100 --json lane.json     # factorial: 10 variants x 12 branches x 2 bots x 2 scenarios (~90 s)
  node qa/scripts/balance_lane.js 100 --sensitivity        # regen 0..3 per branch, + smart bot without self-Haste
  node qa/scripts/balance_lane.js --selfcheck              # instrumentation changes no outcome
  node qa/scripts/control_loops.js 100                     # level-5 control loops vs regen
  node qa/scripts/necro_rotations.js 200                   # Necromancer rotations + level-8 exploit check
  node tools/balance.js --runs 100 --set ...               # per-class numbers below (shipped tool)
  ```
  Raw results for every cell: `qa/results/2026-10-23_balance_lane.json`.

**Observations (evidence):**
1. **1 is enough for Stealth and Combo; 2 is needed for Deception.** At 3, a third or more of turns sit at the cap.
2. **The simple bot does *worse* at 2–3 than at 1 for Assassin and Duelist.** This is a bot artifact. With spare resource it always picks the most expensive skill, Backstab, over Quick Slash, and against the boss's armor Quick Slash hits harder: it auto-crits, averaging 13.0 vs Backstab's 10.4 (measured, level-3 Assassin vs DEF 8), at half the cost. The smart bot is flat at 99–100%. **Side finding:** Backstab is dominated by Quick Slash against armored targets.
3. **"Time 2 makes Chronomancer worse" is the bot's self-Haste habit, not the rate.** Without self-Haste, Chronomancer follows the Illusionist curve exactly (16 → 44 → 98 → 98). Haste costs 2 Time Charges and gives no net action when cast on yourself, so extra regen just funds a wasted Haste every round. This confirms Wren's experiment finding 3. **Haste design (Wren's question 3) and the Time rate are coupled** (see §6).

## 6. Exploit / runaway check
- **Level 3 (today's dungeon):** none. 0 fights hit the 300-action cap in any of the 48,000 factorial runs. A stun-lock policy changes no result, because Shield Bash, the only level-3 stun, holds its QA-2 limit.
- **Level 5 (`control_loops.js`, boss alone, CR off):** re-apply the control whenever it's affordable and the boss isn't already under it.

| illusionist (`confuse`, 2, 50% chance) | 0 *(shipped)* / 1 / **2** | 16% / 5% / **38%** | — | 1 / 2 / **11** | 0 / 89 / 19 (lock) |
| assassin (Garrote silence, 2) | 0 *(shipped)* / 1 / **2–3** | — | 0% / 4% / **91%** | — | 33 / 100 / 100 |

- **Time ≥ 2 lets `slow` deny every boss turn.** The fight stalls because `slow` uses your only action. Two things keep that from becoming a free win today: self-Haste gives no net action, and the Chronomancer has no passive damage source. **If either changes** (Haste granting a real extra action, or a DoT, summon or ally), Time 2 becomes a guaranteed win. CR, Q-04, is the general protection, and it's off. QA-2's anti-chain rule covers Shield Bash only.
- **Stealth ≥ 2 funds Garrote every round:** the boss is reduced to its basic attack 91% of the time. Silence's hard/soft classification is pending under CR.
- **Necromancer turtle loop:** see §7.

## 7. Necromancer isolation (`necro_rotations.js`, Bone Warden level 3, 200 matched seeds)
Bone Wall is learned at level 3, which a no-cheat run reaches right before the boss, so **the boss is the only fight where it exists today.**

**Boss fight, shipped economy:**
| Rotation | Win % (95% CI) | Rounds | Walls | Hits absorbed | Your damage | Damage taken | Soul Energy income earned / forfeited per fight |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| direct damage | **30 (24–36)** | 23.2 | 0 | 0 | 128 | 175 | 19.9 / **3.0** |
| summon-first (banks with the free attack) | 16 (11–21) | 27.4 | 0 | 0 | 107 | 179 | 19.8 / 7.4 |
| Bone Wall every round | **0 (0–2)** | 37.0 | 12.5 | 12.5 | 75 | 183 | 18.7 / **18.3** |
| Bone Wall every other round | 0 (0–2) | 33.6 | 9.4 | 9.4 | 88 | 183 | 19.0 / 14.6 |
| Bone Wall, keeping 1 SE for a bolt | 0 (0–2) | 36.0 | 11.6 | 11.6 | 75 | 183 | 18.5 / 17.5 |

**Unit values (direct, shipped):** an average landed boss hit is **8.6 HP**; an average damaging action deals **6.3**.

**Findings (evidence):**
- **The double opportunity cost is real.** Each wall spends 1 Soul Energy and forfeits the round's +1, because income needs *your* damage. Forfeits rise from 3.0 to 18.3 per fight.
- **The life saved doesn't offset the loss.** A wall saves about 8.6 HP, but your damage per round more than halves (5.5 → 2.0–2.6), the fight lasts 10–14 rounds longer, and total damage taken *rises* (183 vs 175).
- **Summons don't help here.** A skeleton's 5–7 hits are absorbed by the boss's DEF 8 (3 damage per fight).
- **Wren's pending summon damage override** isn't merged. This is the current baseline; I'll re-run if the override changes the skeleton's numbers.

**Scratch hypotheses** (data-only, not approved):
| Rotation | Shipped | + flat 1 SE / round | DEF +2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| direct damage | 30 | 30 | 75 |
| summon-first | 16 | 33 | 67 |
| Bone Wall every other round | 0 | **39** | 14 |
| Bone Wall every round | 0 | 0 (stalls: 300 walls, 0 damage) | 2 |

**Exploit check: level 8 Gravekeeper, one ghoul, then Bone Wall every round:**
| Economy | Win % | Walls | Damage taken (whole fight) | Ghoul damage | Your damage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| shipped | 100 | 14.2 | 47 | 89 | 61 |
| **+ flat 1 SE / round** | 100 | **46.9** | **6** | 146 | 4 |

With unconditional income, the wall pays for itself every round, a single attacker never lands a hit, and the minion wins. Under the shipped economy this can't be sustained, because walling earns nothing.

**Alternative trigger** ("+1 per round in which you acted"): can't be measured without engine code, so per #40 it isn't.
- **For the turtle loop it's exact:** that rotation deals almost no player damage, so "acted" income equals the flat +1. **The exploit result applies to that trigger too.**
- **For mixed rotations, flat +1 is an upper bound:** it also stacks with the damage +1. From the forfeits above, the trigger would restore **11.6 Soul Energy per boss fight** to the every-other-round rotation (14.6 → 3.0 forfeited).
- **Test-only harness work to measure it exactly needs separate authorization.**

## 9. Limitations
- **Bots, not people.** The two policies diverge on Haste and expensive-vs-cheap casts; both are shown.
- **Full-run resource metrics** mostly reflect the base resource, since branches only appear at level 3, right before the boss. Boss-alone is the clean branch lens.
- **Nothing past level 3 is in the factorial.** Levels 5 and 8 appear only in the exploit checks.
- **Boss-alone healing differs from Wren's experiment:** report #1's 77 HP of items vs his 90 HP. Absolute values are lower here; the conclusions are the same, and his full-run table reproduces exactly (report #4).
- **"Resource at the end of rounds"** isn't separately reported: average resource at your turn start and income lost to the cap cover it.
- **Q-04 (CR numbers):** there are no candidate values to measure. §6 shows where CR is load-bearing: Time ≥ 2, Stealth ≥ 2, and future enemy stuns.
