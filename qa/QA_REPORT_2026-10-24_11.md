# QA Report 2026-10-24 #11 — Tokelo's playthrough bugs, after the Mana Seed art (on `main` f15b865)

*Tally — QA & Balance Tester.* Report only: Tokelo asked for Wren to do the fixes.

**Build.** `main` at f15b865, which has PR #157 (Mana Seed character and environments), #158 (water, chest) and #159 (hair colours). There are no open PRs, so this is the build Tokelo plays. `npm test`: **371/371 pass.**

**Method.**
- Tokelo reported three bugs. I reproduced each one in the real `world.html` in headless Chromium, which plays the **16 px world** (`scale16.doubleWorld`), with real key presses.
- I then audited the 16 px geometry of every map, played Chapter One by keys from the cellar to the Warden, and tried five routes through Shamefield in different orders, headless.
- **Update, same day:** Tokelo sent a screenshot of the Captain (Lv 1; the objective box still says "Read the cracked inscription in the cellar"). I reproduced that exact state with `qa/scripts/ch1_skip_cellar.js`. That revises QA-36 and adds QA-39 and QA-40.
- The scripts are in `qa/scripts/` (`playthrough_16px.js`, `geometry_16px.js`, `ch1_orders.js`, `ch1_skip_cellar.js`). The screenshots are in `qa/results/2026-10-24_playthrough/`.

## Summary
1. **You walk through NPCs and enemies (QA-34, confirmed, major).**
   - The 16 px conversion turns every 32 px tile into a 2×2 block. People and enemies are still drawn in the middle of that block, but they only block **one** of its four cells. This holds for **24 of 24** NPCs and enemies.
   - So you can walk into three quarters of every sprite.
   - From below or from the right, you have to step *into* the sprite before E or contact works.
2. **Required fights can be skipped (QA-33, new, major).** The same conversion makes every one-tile choke point two cells wide, but each gate enemy only blocks one cell.
   - All **four** required fights can be walked past: Ragpickers and Root-Chewers on the tollway, the cultists in the orchard and the ghoul on Featherwatch.
   - If you skip them, you reach the Warden with **135 XP (L2) instead of 250 (L3)**, and with no branch choice.
3. **Trees hide the paths (QA-35, confirmed, major).** PR #157 fixed tree crowns covering *people*, but the problem Tokelo sees is the canopy covering the *ground*.
   - Each Mana Seed tree is an 80×112 sprite on a 32 px cell, painted into the static floor layer. It spills 24 px into each neighbour and about 2.3 cells upward.
   - So any path through a tree line is painted over, and on Featherwatch the whole path is invisible.
4. **The Watch Captain blocker (QA-36): confirmed.** Tokelo's screenshot shows the cause: Tokelo never read the cracked inscription in the cellar.
   - The main quests form one chain, Q01 → Q02 → … → Q05. Each quest starts only when the one before it completes, and each objective counts only once it is the current one.
   - So with Q01 stuck on its first objective, Q02 to Q05 never start. Elian, Tamsin and Mara all talk normally, but their talks don't count yet.
   - The Captain only has his default line, *"Keep the peace and we'll get along."*, and the gate stays shut.
   - **Recoverable but invisible:** go back and read the inscription. Then look at the foundation stone and step onto the road, and everything you already did is credited at once. In my run that was Q01 to Q04 and the Captain's objective, for 85 XP in one go. That is the "extra XP from Elian" Tokelo saw.
5. **The world never stops you from outrunning the main quest (QA-39, new, major).** Nothing physical holds you to the chain: every exit from the cellar to Shamefield is open with Q01 still active. The only lock is the orchard gate, so the chapter "breaks" at the Captain, a long way from the cause.
   - The only pointer is the objective box, which still says "→ The Cellar" while you stand in the village.
6. **Optional content ignores the main quest (QA-40, design question, minor).** Shane's whole quest, including the constable fight and the 50 XP, can be started and finished while the main quest is still on the cellar. That's because S01 opens on simply entering Shamefield.

## For Wren (suggestions, not decisions)
- **QA-33 and QA-34 share one cause:** objects keep one cell when `doubleMap` turns tiles into 2×2 blocks. Two possible fixes:
  - Give each placed object a footprint, so that it blocks, and can be talked to or fought from, every cell of its block.
  - Or place objects so that the cell they block is the one they're drawn on, and widen gates to the full doorway.
- **QA-35:** keep the canopy off walkable path tiles in the floor layer. Draw the parts of the crown that spill over paths in the overhead layer with some transparency, or use a narrower tree piece next to paths.
- **QA-37:** run `validateWorld` and the gate checks on `doubleWorld(zone1)` too, so this can't come back silently.
