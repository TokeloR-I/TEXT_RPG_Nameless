# QA Report 2026-10-23 #7 — Rook's Necromancer passive audit (PR #58 at 1629b6a, on `main` f0b7184)

*Tally — QA & Balance Tester.* Rook asked for an independent review of the passives verified in PR #58, and for a check of the exact Grave Command gap. PR #58 adds one test file (`tests/19-necromancer-passive-audit.test.js`) and changes no engine code.

## Summary
- **Merge status:** PR #58 merges cleanly and the merged tree passes **167/167**. It is **not ready to merge yet, for two test problems:**
  - **The Plague Reservoir test proves nothing (QA-26).** It still passes with Plague Reservoir deleted from the engine.
  - **The Bone Fortress test locks in behaviour that contradicts the trait's own text (QA-25).**
- **Confirmed** through real levelling, with each trait earned by levelling up and never pushed in by hand:
  - **Bone Armor:** 2% per live minion, for physical and magic damage; true damage ignores it.
  - **Pestilent Sage:** +1 duration for Contagion, Black Spit and other Poison; stacks unchanged.
  - **Plague Reservoir:** exactly +1 Soul Energy on your own kill of an infected enemy.
  - **Harbinger:** a flat +2 per Infection tick.
- **Found: Bone Fortress does nothing from level 8 to 13 (QA-25).** The trait text says "+1% dmg reduction per minion (max 15%)", but the engine only lifts the cap from 10% to 15%. Before Black Pharaoh (L14) you can have at most 3 minions, so the reduction tops out at 6%, with or without the trait.
- **Gaps confirmed, exactly as Rook describes:**
  - Grave Command: the skeleton attacks enemy 1 every time.
  - Ossified Champion: skeletons keep 15 HP and 5–7 damage.
  - Death Knight: no +25%.
  - Channel the Grave: the 1-turn cooldown is not enforced.
- **Input for N-7:** an **Infection tick kill already pays Plague Reservoir**, because the tick's source is the caster. Only minion kills don't pay.

Repro for everything below: `ENSO_ROOT=<checkout> node qa/scripts/necro_passives.js`. The output is identical on `main` and on PR #58: 5/7 pass, 3 confirmed gaps, 4 info.
