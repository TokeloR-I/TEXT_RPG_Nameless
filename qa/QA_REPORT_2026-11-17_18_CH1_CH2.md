# QA report #18: Chapters One and Two end to end, and the new Chapter Two enemies

**Tally (QA) with Wren (dev fixes)** · 2026-11-17 · `main` at `f783ab3` (#269). Requested by Tokelo ("can Tally do some
testing for new area and chapter 1 and 2").

Covered: every quest in Chapters One and Two, every map, the Chapter Two enemies and side quests from #269 (bog lights,
mire-dead, the Man in the Peat, drowned oarsmen, Wolf-Coats; C2-S03, C2-S11, C2-S12), and every Chapter Two enemy
group in live combat. Boss difficulty and class balance were left alone (a separate thread is making bosses harder).

## Summary

- **Both chapters can be finished.** A new story crawler (`qa/scripts/story_crawl.js`) plays the game with no script:
  it walks every map it can reach, talks to everyone, tries every dialogue choice, inspects, picks up and fights.
  On every class and several seeds it reaches **all 30 maps** and finishes **42 of 44 quests**. The two left are by
  design: CB2 (James's deal with Demonkore, marked "not written yet") and QS1 (the Fallen Seraphim's trial, which needs
  the 120 s gaze and level 10). C2-S03, C2-S11 and C2-S12 finish on every run.
- **Four bugs found, three fixed here** (below). The fourth changes boss difficulty, so it went to the boss thread.
- Tests 848/848 before, **852/852 after** (4 new in `tests/108-qa18-live-fights.test.js`, each fails on the old code).
  Golden replays unchanged (`31be6227b5ca719f / c4a1232b3a9400a5`, world `deec36031435bef6 / 16a2e809e3c4471d`).
  Every map reachable on foot (`world_reach.js`, 30 maps). The action fuzzer broke no invariant once its stale
  save check was fixed.

## Handed on (not changed here)

- **Boss thread (Make bosses Dark Souls hard): the big bosses get stuck on corners.** The four radius-10 bosses
  (Crow Warden, Bjarki the Grave-Bound, the Binding Echo, the Heaven Doorwarden) path through one-tile gaps their body
  can't pass. They press into the corner and never move again, and since they have a path they never count as "lost",
  so the fight never ends either. A ranged player behind a pillar can farm them. A patch (body-aware `findPath`) went
  to that thread and is being merged there. With it, the Warden benchmark (20 seeds) moves mage illusionist 65% to 40%,
  elementalist 70% to 25% and bone warden 95% to 75%: much of the casters' Warden win rate was this cheese.
- **Gauge: Chapter Two is easy at level 6.** With the bot (`qa/scripts/ch2_live.js`, results in
  `qa/results/ch2_live_2026-11-17.md`), every Chapter Two group except Bjarki is won 100% by all four classes in 6–37 s,
  the Man in the Peat in about 20 s. This matches Tokelo's playtester ("a bit too easy"). It is a numbers call, not a bug.
- **Seen, harmless:** in a few conversations the step only completes on the "Goodbye." choice (Ketil's "told" node,
  C2-S11). Leaving with Esc instead doesn't lose the step; talking to him again shows the same node.

## Stale QA harnesses fixed

- `action_fuzz.js` reported "restorePosition failed" on Skeldvik. The game passes the save's content revision so a
  re-laid map (Skeldvik, #251) restarts at its spawn on purpose; the harness didn't pass it. It does now.

## New QA tools

| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| `qa/scripts/story_crawl.js [seed] [class] [--verbose]` | Plays both chapters with no script (forced wins) and lists every quest that never finished, with the step it is stuck on. Kitchen, farm and build menu steps are done for it and listed as "assisted". |
| `qa/scripts/story_audit.js` | Static checks the tests don't make: item entries for everything handed out, flags read but never set, items asked for but never obtainable, quests never started, markers far from their target. Clean now. |
| `qa/scripts/ch2_live.js [--seeds 4] [--level 6] [--only id] [--class c]` | Every Chapter Two group alone on its map, fought live by the warden_benchmark bot for each class. Reports win rate, time, timeouts with the enemies left and where they stand. |
