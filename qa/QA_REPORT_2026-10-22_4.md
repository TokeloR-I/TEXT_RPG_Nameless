## QA-1 — opportunity-based expiry (`qa1_expiry.js` 19/19, `speed_matrix.js`)
Speed matrix, one enemy, player faster / slower: Marked for Death **1/1**, Expose **2/2**, Guard **1/1** (were 0/1, 1/2, 2/1). Evasion, Riposte, Taunt, Stun, Silence, Blind and Bleed are unchanged, as the decision requires.

Checked against the handoff's matrix with my own scenarios:
- **Marked for Death:** a 0-damage hit or a dodge doesn't use it up. Flurry boosts only the first hit. An allied skeleton can consume it; an enemy hitting a player-marked enemy can't. Re-marking gives no second charge. An unused mark expires at the end of your next turn, in both speed orders.
- **Expose:** all 6 Flurry hits across the 2-turn window are boosted, none after it. A refresh restarts the full window.
- **Guard:** with 3 enemies, exactly the 3 hits before your next turn are reduced, whether you're faster or slower.
- **Turn edge cases:** Haste bonus actions don't shorten Expose or end Guard. A stunned turn doesn't burn Marked for Death. Guard ends at the start of a skipped turn.
- **Cleanup:** cleanse removes the statuses at once; victory clears everything.
- **Applier-death ruling:** an enemy-applied Expose outlives its dead applier until the end of that round (still boosts that round's hits), then expires, including when the applier dies before acting. Weaken from a dead applier keeps the normal round rule. A same-round Hollow King revival doesn't cancel the scheduled expiry.

## QA-2 — Shield Bash (`qa2_shieldbash.js` 14/14)
- **Threshold (criteria 1–4):** Oath 0/1/2 → damage, no stun; 3 and 7 → stun. Oath Points are never spent (4 → 4). A dodged or absorbed bash gives no stun and starts no lockout.
- **One-turn control (5):** exactly one skipped turn, in both speed orders. A Haste bonus action doesn't count as the target's recovery turn.
- **Anti-lock (6):** 30 bashes at ≥3 Oath, CR off → the boss's turns go `SASASA…` in both speed orders, never two skips in a row.
- **Report #1's QA-2 repro with honest Oath Points** (Guard twice, then bash): the boss acted **18 times and was skipped 15**. In report #1 it acted once and was skipped 20 times.
- **Control Resistance with test values (7–9):** stuns build CR; the stun that reaches 100 lands; later ones are RESISTED until release. Threshold failure, lockout, immunity and a blind miss add no CR. The boss's first stun lands even at 120 CR.
- **Regressions and cleanup (10–11):** Guard still gives +1 Oath. Reaper's Charge (another stun source) gets no lockout. The lockout state is cleared between fights.

## Dragon's Dominion (`dominion.js` 9/10 + 2 info)
- Learned at Wyrm's End through real progression and spends all Fury ("Used 7 Fury Gauge").
- ×3 final damage against the boss, both true and physical; ordinary enemies unaffected. An enemy holding it gets no bonus against the non-boss player.
- "For 1 turn" means your next turn is tripled and the one after isn't, in both speed orders. Haste bonus actions are tripled and don't shorten it.
- Stacks multiplicatively with Expose (20 → 72). Once per fight, usable again next fight, cleared on victory.
- The FAIL is QA-15. The skill can't be reached in normal play (it needs level 14 and a story event that doesn't exist yet).
